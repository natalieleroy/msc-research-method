**Why this clip is here:** MINE, not the paper's. Three things constrain how DICE can be used. One-sided testing presupposes the direction of the effect being tested, and with no comparator the p-values carry no inferential weight - cite the d values descriptively and never say DICE "worked". Regression to the mean and expectancy alone would produce this pattern in 10 people given four sessions and daily prompts. And the exclusion of pronounced delusional symptoms means the people the intervention matters most for were not in it. The exacerbation dropout is worth one honest sentence: an intervention that asks someone to attend repeatedly to their delusional thinking is not risk-free.
