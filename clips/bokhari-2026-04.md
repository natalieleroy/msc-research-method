**Why this clip is here:** ATTRIBUTION, now resolved: this is Burns et al. (2025), 'The algorithm is hacked', British Journal of Psychiatry, advance online publication, and the PDF is in sources/. Cite Burns directly. Three things the primary shows that this quote does not. (1) The familiar "OR 1.15, 95% CI 1.01-1.31" is BOKHARI'S exponentiation: Burns report a bare OR of 1.15 with a confidence interval on the log-odds coefficient, 95% CI (0.008, 0.270). The arithmetic is right but the interval is not Burns' published figure, so present it as derived or quote the log-odds. (2) The denominator matters: 51.7% is of the 201 patients WITH delusional content, i.e. 104 - which is 45.6% of the 228 in the study. Burns' own abstract reads as "over half" of all subjects, and repeating that overstates it. (3) Burns' Table 1 splits the cohort 154 and 77, which sums to 231 rather than 228, and the 77 does not reconcile with the 104 in their Results; the paper offers no footnote explaining it, so their conclusion that no demographic variable predicts technology delusions rests on a split they never explain. Say so if you use that conclusion.
