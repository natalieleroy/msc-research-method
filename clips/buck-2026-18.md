**Why this clip is here:** The instrument the delusion findings rest on, and worth describing because the two-part structure is conceptually neat: delusions ABOUT AI, and delusions MAINTAINED BY AI. Be careful how you report its psychometrics - exploratory factor analysis on the same sample that generated the items is not validation, and Cronbach alpha of 0.95 on 16 items may indicate redundancy as much as coherence. The honest line is that this is a new, promising, not yet independently validated measure.
