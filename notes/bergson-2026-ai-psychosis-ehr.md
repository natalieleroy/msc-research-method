# Bergson, Z., Vassall, S. G., Wright, A., McCoy, A. B., Schafer, K. M., Achee, M. C. & Sheffield, J. M. (2026)

**Characterizing Artificial Intelligence Psychosis in a Large Medical Setting**

DOI: 10.1001/jamapsychiatry.2026.2923

## How much weight it bears: moderate

The first systematic clinical-sample characterisation of AI psychosis: a retrospective EHR review across 578,058 records at a large US academic centre, giving the only prevalence estimate in the corpus. Constrained by single-site design, ascertainment bias (it can only see what clinicians wrote down), and a typology rating system the authors say is not clinically validated.

*Graded on design-only.*
