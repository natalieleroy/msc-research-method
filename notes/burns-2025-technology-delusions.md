# Burns, A. V., Nelson, K., Wang, H., Hegarty, E. M. & Cohn, A. B. (2025)

**'The algorithm is hacked': analysis of technology delusions in a modern-day cohort**

DOI: 10.1192/bjp.2025.10452

## How much weight it bears: moderate

A single-centre retrospective chart review of 228 adults with psychosis that supplies the only year-on-year trend estimate in the corpus for technology-themed delusional content, and rich verbatim description of its subtypes. It can establish that technology themes are common in a contemporary psychosis service and that they became more likely across 2016-2024 in this sample; it cannot establish population prevalence, cause, or that technology contributed to the delusions, because the sample is a selected, housed, insured, substance-abstinent day-programme cohort and the data were never collected for research. The tier is held at moderate rather than higher by the design, by the authors' own point that rising clinician awareness could itself produce the trend, and by an unexplained discrepancy between the group sizes in Table 1 and the counts in the Results text (see Limitations).

*Graded on full-appraisal.*
