# Cannon, B. J. & Kramer, L. M. (2012)

**Delusion content across the 20th century in an American psychiatric hospital**

DOI: 10.1177/0020764010396413

## How much weight it bears: indicative

A retrospective archival study of 102 records, sampled by decade from a single US state psychiatric hospital, which can show that delusional content in an American sample tracked sociocultural change in the same way non-American samples had. It cannot support any statement about frequency in a population, nor about technology specifically, because the sample is tiny per decade, confined to one institution, and the abstract reports no technology-specific result at all. Indicative rather than moderate because of the per-decade cell sizes and because the appraisal rests on the abstract alone.

*Graded on design-only.*
