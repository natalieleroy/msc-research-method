# Cantor-Graae, E. & Selten, J. P. (2005)

**Schizophrenia and migration: a meta-analysis and review.**

DOI: 10.1176/appi.ajp.162.1.12

## How much weight it bears: moderate

A mixed-effects meta-analysis of population-based incidence studies that established the size of the migrant excess in schizophrenia — relative risk 2.7 (95% CI 2.3-3.2) for first-generation and 4.5 (95% CI 1.5-13.1) for second-generation migrants — and found it graded by the migrant group's region of origin and skin colour, which is the finding that pushed the field towards psychosocial rather than genetic explanations. It cannot establish causation and the second-generation estimate rests on only 7 effect sizes with an interval from 1.5 to 13.1. Moderate rather than strong because the search was MEDLINE-only, English-language-only, and now over twenty years out of date; its figures are superseded by later reviews and it should be cited as the foundational finding rather than the current estimate.

*Graded on design-only.*
