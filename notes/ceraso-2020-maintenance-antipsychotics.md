# Ceraso, A., Lin, J. J., Schneider-Thoma, J., Siafis, S., Tardy, M., Komossa, K., Heres, S., Kissling, W., Davis, J. M. & Leucht, S. (2020)

**Maintenance treatment with antipsychotic drugs for schizophrenia.**

DOI: 10.1002/14651858.CD008016.pub3

## How much weight it bears: strong

A Cochrane review of 75 placebo-controlled RCTs and 9145 participants with GRADE certainty ratings, reporting the relapse-prevention effect as high-certainty evidence: 24% relapse on drug versus 61% on placebo at seven to twelve months (RR 0.38, 95% CI 0.32 to 0.45, NNTB 3), with hospitalisation reduced from 18% to 7% (RR 0.43, 95% CI 0.32 to 0.57, NNTB 8). It also quantifies the harms in the same currency — movement disorders 14% versus 8% (RR 1.52, NNTH 20), sedation and weight gain — which is what makes it usable for a shared decision rather than an argument for medication. It CANNOT answer the vignette's actual question: the population is schizophrenia broadly rather than first episode, the evidence runs 'approximately up to two years of follow-up' and no further, the suicide and employment analyses are underpowered and low-certainty, and randomisation, allocation and blinding were 'poorly reported' in many included studies. Strong on design and on the authors' own GRADE rating of the primary outcome.

*Graded on design-only.*
