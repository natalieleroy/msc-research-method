**Prenatal and perinatal risk and protective factors for psychosis: a systematic review and meta-analysis.**

DOI: 10.1016/S2215-0366(20)30057-2

## How much weight it bears: strong

A PROSPERO-registered (CRD42017079261) systematic review and meta-analysis of 152 non-overlapping cohort and case-control datasets covering 98 candidate factors, with random-effects pairwise meta-analyses, correction for publication bias, sensitivity analyses, meta-regressions and a study-quality assessment. It can establish which prenatal and perinatal exposures are reliably associated with later psychosis and how large each association is. It cannot establish causation or mechanism, and because almost every individual odds ratio is small — most between 1.2 and 1.9 — it cannot support a strong single-factor neurodevelopmental claim.

*Graded on design-only.*
