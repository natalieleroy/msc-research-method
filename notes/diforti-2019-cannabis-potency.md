**The contribution of cannabis use to variation in the incidence of psychotic disorder across Europe (EU-GEI): a multicentre case-control study.**

DOI: 10.1016/S2215-0366(19)30048-3

## How much weight it bears: strong

A multicentre first-episode case-control study (901 cases, 1237 population controls) across 11 sites, which links individual-level exposure to site-level incidence and so does something a single-site case-control cannot: it tests whether differences in cannabis use patterns track differences in incidence between places. It can establish a graded association by frequency and potency and estimate how much of the incidence at each site is attributable to high-potency use. It cannot establish causation — the population attributable fractions are explicitly calculated 'assuming causality' — and the site-level correlations rest on only 11 data points.

*Graded on design-only.*
