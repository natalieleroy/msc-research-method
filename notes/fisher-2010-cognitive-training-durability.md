# Fisher, M., Holland, C., Subramaniam, K. & Vinogradov, S. (2010)

**Neuroplasticity-based cognitive training in schizophrenia: An interim report on the effects 6 months later.**

DOI: 10.1093/schbul/sbn170

## How much weight it bears: indicative

A randomised trial, which is the strongest DESIGN in this part of the corpus, but an interim report on 32 participants split unevenly between arms (22 training, 10 computer-games control), with the training arm itself split 12/10 by dose. It can establish that durable cognitive gains at 6 months after neuroplasticity-based training are demonstrable and that the direction of effect favours training. It cannot establish efficacy at any useful precision: the arms are far too small, the authors describe it as interim and ongoing, and the functional-outcome improvement is explicitly non-significant. Tier is indicative, not moderate, because of sample size and interim status, not because of design. Sixteen years old, and the training software has been superseded, so it is FOUNDATIONAL for the mechanism and not current for efficacy.
