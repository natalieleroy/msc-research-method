**Cognitive interventions targeting brain plasticity in the prodromal and early phases of schizophrenia.**

DOI: 10.1146/annurev-clinpsy-032511-143134

## How much weight it bears: indicative

An invited Annual Review that can establish the standard conceptual apparatus of early intervention — prodrome, clinical high risk, ultra high risk, duration of untreated psychosis, Birchwood's 'critical period' — with the terms attributed to their originators, and can summarise the state of cognitive therapy and cognitive training trials as of 2013. It cannot carry any prevalence, transition-rate or efficacy figure as current: it is thirteen years old and this corpus already holds Salazar de Pablo et al. (2021) on CHR transition, which supersedes it on that number. Tier is indicative: authoritative narrative review, no primary data, no systematic search.
