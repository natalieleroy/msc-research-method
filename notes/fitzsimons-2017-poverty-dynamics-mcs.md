# Fitzsimons, E., Goodman, A., Kelly, E. & Smith, J. P. (2017)

**Poverty dynamics and parental mental health: Determinants of childhood mental health in the UK.**

DOI: 10.1016/j.socscimed.2016.12.040

## How much weight it bears: moderate

A large national birth cohort followed to age 11, which is a genuinely longitudinal design and therefore scarce in this corpus. It can establish that both persistent and transitory poverty are associated with all four SDQ domains at ages 5 and 11, with somewhat stronger estimated associations for persistent poverty, and that parental mental health is a major correlate. It CANNOT establish anything about psychosis: the outcome is the Strengths and Difficulties Questionnaire and the related Rutter scale, which screen for hyperactivity, emotional symptoms, conduct problems and peer problems, and the cohort is far too young for psychosis onset. It also cannot establish causation, being observational. Tier is moderate on quality; its FIT for a psychosis essay is POOR.
