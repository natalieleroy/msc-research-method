# Flathers, M., Roux, S. & Torous, J. (2026)

**Beyond artificial intelligence psychosis: a functional typology of large language model-associated psychotic phenomena**

DOI: 10.1016/j.landig.2025.100974

## How much weight it bears: indicative

A peer-reviewed Lancet Digital Health Viewpoint that proposes the catalyst / amplifier / coauthor / object typology for how LLM systems figure in psychotic phenomena, co-written by a software engineer, a person with lived experience of schizophrenia and a psychiatrist. It can establish where the typology comes from and what each category means; it cannot establish that the four roles are exhaustive, mutually exclusive or empirically distinguishable, because it reports no data and the illustrative cases are drawn from press accounts. Indicative rather than weak because it is peer reviewed in a major venue and has already been applied as a coding scheme by an independent team.

*Graded on design-only.*
