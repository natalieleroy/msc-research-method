**Real-world comprehensive care of people living with schizophrenia: recommendations across different settings and clinical stages.**

DOI: 10.1002/wps.70066

## How much weight it bears: moderate

The first set of recommendations for real-world comprehensive care of schizophrenia stratified by resource setting and clinical stage, published in World Psychiatry by a 35-author panel including clinicians practising in India, South Africa, Chile, Brazil, Colombia, Uganda, Tunisia, Mexico, Malawi and Russia, with some experts by experience reviewing the materials. It can establish what the field currently recommends across eleven domains, that those recommendations differ by resource setting, and - usefully for a sceptical reading - where the authors themselves say the evidence is not yet good enough, as they do for digital monitoring technologies. It cannot establish efficacy of anything: there is no systematic search protocol, no PROSPERO registration, no formal grading of evidence, no stated number of experts and no described consensus procedure beyond 'collaborative appraisal', so it is expert opinion built on a critical review and must be cited as a recommendation rather than as evidence. Rated moderate on venue, breadth, transparency about its own uncertainty and the inclusion of non-Western practice, and held below strong by the absence of any stated method for either the search or the consensus.
