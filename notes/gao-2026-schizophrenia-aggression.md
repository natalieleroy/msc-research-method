# Gao, Q., Zhu, P. & Hu, H. (2026)

**Factors associated with aggressive behavior in patients with schizophrenia: a systematic review and meta-analysis.**

DOI: 10.1186/s40359-025-03936-x

## How much weight it bears: moderate

A PROSPERO-registered (CRD420251147415), PRISMA-compliant meta-analysis searching ten databases, six English and four Chinese (CNKI, Wanfang, VIP, CBM), 2014 to October 2024, with Newcastle-Ottawa quality scoring (mostly 7-9 of 9), sensitivity and subgroup analyses, and funnel plots. It can establish the strength of association between a defined set of correlates and aggression in people already diagnosed with schizophrenia, with pooled odds ratios and confidence intervals. It cannot establish causation or anything about the onset of psychosis: the authors state that the included studies were 'primarily case-control and cross-sectional studies, which limit causal inference regarding etiology', and the outcome 'aggressive behavior' had no standardised operational definition across the primary studies. Current, not foundational. Fit caveat, which matters more here than the tier: this is violence risk in established schizophrenia, a different construct and a different population from first-episode or clinical high-risk psychosis, so it cannot carry any predisposing-factor claim in Essay 1.

*Graded on full-appraisal.*
