# Gonzalo, O., Sanchez-Gonzalez, J. L., Sanchez-Gil, A., Carrascal-Laso, L., Isidoro-Garcia, M., Martin-Vallejo, J. & Perez, J. (2026)

**Evidence-based interventions for vocational recovery in first-episode psychosis: A meta-analysis of employment and education rates.**

DOI: 10.1177/00048674261471316

## How much weight it bears: moderate

A PROSPERO-registered PRISMA meta-analysis of 31 studies (n=8,651) with formal risk-of-bias assessment (RoB2 and ROBINS-E) and GRADE certainty rating, analysing employment, education and combined vocational rates both as pooled proportions and as intervention-versus-control comparisons. It can establish that vocational intervention improves competitive employment after a first episode (overall OR 2.35, 95% CI 1.88-2.94; Individual Placement and Support OR 2.86, 95% CI 1.81-4.54) and - the more useful half for this project - that no intervention showed a consistent advantage for EDUCATION rates, with a confidence interval that includes no effect (OR 1.19, 95% CI 0.80-1.76). It cannot establish much about the combined endpoint, which the authors themselves describe as limited in interpretability by substantial heterogeneity (I-squared 69.6%, tau-squared 0.296, p<0.001) and possible publication bias. Rated moderate rather than strong because the full text could not be reached, so the GRADE certainty ratings, the per-outcome risk-of-bias judgements, the countries of included studies and the definitions of 'education' are all unverified, and because an online-first record carries no volume, issue or page numbers.

*Graded on design-only.*
