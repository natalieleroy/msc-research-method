# Hakamata, Y., Suzuki, Y., Kobashikawa, H. & Hori, H. (2022)

**Neurobiology of early life adversity: A systematic review of meta-analyses towards an integrative account of its neurobiological trajectories to mental disorders.**

DOI: 10.1016/j.yfrne.2022.100994

## How much weight it bears: moderate

A PRISMA-guided systematic review of 27 meta-analyses that can establish convergent neurobiological correlates of adverse childhood experiences across four previously separate literatures — HPA axis, inflammation, neuroimaging and genetics/epigenetics — and can establish that those correlates appear in people with and without a psychiatric diagnosis, which is a real and unusual finding. It cannot establish causation and the authors call for longitudinal work to do so; it is also a review of reviews, so it is three steps from any participant and inherits every constituent meta-analysis's bias. Its outcome is mental disorders in general, NOT psychosis, which limits its fit here. Tier is moderate: rigorous method, stated limitations, but an umbrella design on a non-specific outcome.
