# Hjorthøj, C., Compton, W., Starzer, M., Nordholm, D., Einstein, E., Erlangsen, A., Nordentoft, M., Volkow, N. D. & Han, B. (2023)

**Association between cannabis use disorder and schizophrenia stronger in young males than in females.**

DOI: 10.1017/S0033291723000880

## How much weight it bears: strong

A nationwide register cohort covering 6,907,859 people and 129,521,260 person-years with 45,327 incident schizophrenia cases, which effectively removes selection and recall bias from exposure and outcome measurement and is large enough to estimate sex- and age-stratified effects and population attributable fractions over fifty years. It can establish that the association between diagnosed cannabis use disorder and schizophrenia is graded by sex and age and has grown over time. It cannot establish causation, and the exposure is a recorded diagnosis of cannabis use disorder rather than cannabis use, so it captures the severe tail of use and nothing below it.

*Graded on design-only.*
