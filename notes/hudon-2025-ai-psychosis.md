# Hudon, A. & Stip, E. (2025)

**Delusional Experiences Emerging From AI Chatbot Interactions or 'AI Psychosis'**

DOI: 10.2196/85799

## How much weight it bears: indicative

A peer-reviewed Viewpoint in JMIR Mental Health that defines AI psychosis as a heuristic framework rather than a diagnostic entity and maps it onto the stress-vulnerability model, the digital therapeutic alliance and theory-of-mind accounts. It can establish how the term is used in the published literature and supply a conceptual vocabulary for a mechanism; it cannot establish that the mechanism operates, how often, or in whom, because it reports no data and the authors state the pathways are heuristic and make no causal claim. Indicative rather than weak because it is peer reviewed, explicitly scoped, and the authors are psychiatrists writing within established psychosis models, but it remains opinion rather than evidence.
