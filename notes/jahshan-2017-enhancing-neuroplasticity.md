# Jahshan, C., Rassovsky, Y. & Green, M. F. (2017)

**Enhancing neuroplasticity to augment cognitive remediation in schizophrenia.**

DOI: 10.3389/fpsyt.2017.00191

## How much weight it bears: indicative

A short mini-review, team-independent of the Vinogradov lineage, that can establish that response to cognitive remediation is highly variable, that combining it with exercise or tDCS is a live research strategy, and that the mechanistic rationale runs through BDNF and long-term potentiation. It cannot establish that the combination works: the authors state there are 'insufficient data to determine if this multimodal approach leads to incremental performance gain in patients' and call for larger RCTs. Nine years old and the tDCS literature has grown substantially since, so any claim about the state of that evidence must be dated. Tier is indicative: a mini-review with no primary data and explicitly insufficient evidence on its own question.

*Graded on full-appraisal.*
