# Jauhar, S., Laws, K. R. & McKenna, P. J. (2019)

**CBT for schizophrenia: a critical viewpoint**

DOI: 10.1017/S0033291718004166

## How much weight it bears: indicative

An editorial 'critical viewpoint', not an independent synthesis: it reports effect sizes drawn from other meta-analyses, one of which is the authors' own (Jauhar et al., 2014), and it has no protocol, no search strategy and no risk-of-bias assessment of its own. It can establish that the pooled effect of CBTp on positive symptoms in meta-analyses published since 2014 lies in the small range, that the effect attenuates under blinding, and that these authors read the evidence as not supporting CBTp for negative symptoms or relapse prevention. It cannot stand as the source of an effect-size figure, because every number in it is secondary; for those, cite Jauhar et al. (2014), British Journal of Psychiatry, 204 (1): 20-29, directly. Indicative rather than moderate because of the publication type and the authors' declared position in the controversy.

*Graded on full-appraisal.*
