# Jones, C., Hacker, D., Meaden, A., Cormac, I., Irving, C. B., Xia, J., Zhao, S., Shi, C. & Chen, J. (2018)

**Cognitive behavioural therapy plus standard care versus standard care plus other psychosocial treatments for people with schizophrenia**

DOI: 10.1002/14651858.CD008712.pub3

## How much weight it bears: strong

A Cochrane review with a registered protocol, a specialist trials register searched without language or publication-status limits, independent duplicate screening and extraction, risk-of-bias assessment and GRADE rating: the strongest design in the corpus for a treatment question. What it establishes is narrow and often misread: it compares CBT against OTHER psychosocial therapies as add-ons, not against standard care alone, and it found no clear difference on relapse, rehospitalisation, mental state, death, social functioning or quality of life. It therefore cannot be cited for the size of CBTp's effect against treatment-as-usual, and the authors themselves rate the evidence as mainly low or very low quality. Strong on design; the fit to the claim it is currently being used for is poor.

*Graded on design-only.*
