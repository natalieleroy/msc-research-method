# Jongsma, H. E., Turner, C., Kirkbride, J. B. & Jones, P. B. (2019)

**International incidence of psychotic disorders, 2002-17: a systematic review and meta-analysis.**

DOI: 10.1016/S2468-2667(19)30056-8

## How much weight it bears: strong

A PROSPERO-registered systematic review (CRD42018086800) screening 56,721 records to 177 included studies, with random-effects meta-analysis and meta-regression that tests study method as an explanation for the variation it finds. It can establish the pooled incidence of psychotic disorders, the size of the male and ethnic-minority excess, and that part of the geographical variation is an artefact of case-ascertainment design. It cannot establish causes, and heterogeneity was extreme (I-squared at or above 98.5%), which means the pooled rate of 26.6 per 100,000 person-years is a summary of a very dispersed distribution rather than a figure that describes any particular place.

*Graded on design-only.*
