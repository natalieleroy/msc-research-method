# Kapur, S. (2003)

**Psychosis as a state of aberrant salience: a framework linking biology, phenomenology, and pharmacology in schizophrenia**

DOI: 10.1176/appi.ajp.160.1.13

## How much weight it bears: indicative

The primary source for aberrant salience: the paper that proposed dopamine's role as mediating the salience of events and representations, and that delusions are the person's own cognitive effort to make sense of aberrantly salient experience while hallucinations are the direct experience of aberrant salience in internal representations. It can establish what the framework claims and where it came from, which is what the corpus needs, because the mechanism is currently cited through `notes/bokhari-2026-technology-delusions.md` and restated in `notes/howes-2009-dopamine-hypothesis-iii.md`. It cannot establish anything empirically: it collects no data, tests nothing, and the author describes it as 'a heuristic framework' and discusses its limitations himself. Indicative is the tier for a theory paper used for a concept - it is foundational and it is not evidence, and no amount of citation makes a framework a finding. Rated from the abstract and from Howes and Kapur's (2009) restatement of it; the full text is paywalled.
