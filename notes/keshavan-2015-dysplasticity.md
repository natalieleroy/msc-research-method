**Dysplasticity, metaplasticity, and schizophrenia: Implications for risk, illness, and novel interventions.**

DOI: 10.1017/S095457941500019X

## How much weight it bears: indicative

A theoretical synthesis, not a study: it can establish that a coherent 'dysplastic' model exists in the peer-reviewed literature and can supply the critical-period vocabulary for discussing when risk converts to onset, but it cannot evidence any individual causal link, because every empirical claim in it is a secondary citation. Used here as FOUNDATIONAL conceptual framing rather than as current evidence; at eleven years old its empirical substrate predates the 2022 GWAS and the current CHR transition meta-analyses already in this corpus. The tier is indicative because it is a theory paper by authors with authority in the field, with no primary data of its own.
