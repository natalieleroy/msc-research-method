## How much weight it bears: strong

A very large multi-country general-population survey that can establish that childhood adversities are highly prevalent, interrelated, and associated with all classes of DSM-IV disorder at all life-course stages in all country-income groups, and that the association is non-specific rather than disorder-specific. It cannot establish causation, and the adversity reports are retrospective and self-reported. Decisively for this project, it CANNOT be used as a second, independent source alongside McGrath et al. (2017): both analyse the same WMH dataset and share Kessler, McLaughlin, Sampson, Aguilar-Gaxiola, Alonso, de Girolamo, Florescu, Gureje, Haro, Hu, Karam, Lee, Lepine, Posada-Villa and Viana as authors. Tier is strong on its own merits as a design; its value here is limited by that dependence.

*Graded on full-appraisal.*
