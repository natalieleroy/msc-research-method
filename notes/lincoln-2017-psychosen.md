# Lincoln, T. & Heibach, E. (2017)

**Psychosen**

DOI: 10.1026/02749-000

## How much weight it bears: not-citable

A German-language practitioner monograph in a psychotherapy textbook series, not a research report: it has no design, no sample and no data of its own, and the proposition Böge cites it for - linking delusions to distress and to impaired functioning socially - is a textbook summary of a primary literature that is itself readily citable. Not-citable in the sense the tier intends: for an English-language MSc essay it is unverifiable by the reader and by the marker, and using it would mean citing a secondary synthesis in a language the essay is not written in for a claim that empirical sources can carry directly. The entry exists so that the chain is documented, not so that the book is cited.

*Graded on publication-status.*
