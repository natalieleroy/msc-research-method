# Lincoln, T. M. & Peters, E. (2019)

**A systematic review and discussion of symptom specific cognitive behavioural approaches to delusions and hallucinations**

DOI: 10.1016/j.schres.2017.12.014

## How much weight it bears: moderate

A systematic review of RCTs published in a specialist journal, which can establish that the small number of trials of symptom-specific CBT for delusions or hallucinations have reported post-therapy effect sizes above d=0.4 on at least one primary outcome, and that this compares favourably with the small-to-moderate range reported for generic CBTp. It cannot establish efficacy, because by the authors' own account only three of twelve trials were adequately powered, the rest were pilots, no meta-analytic pooling or risk-of-bias assessment is reported in the abstract, and follow-up periods were mostly brief. Moderate rather than strong on that basis, and because the record is abstract-only - no pooled estimate, no heterogeneity and no publication-bias check has been inspected.

*Graded on design-only.*
