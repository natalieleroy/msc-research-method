# Lo Buglio, G., Boldrini, T., Polari, A., Fiorentino, F., Nelson, B., Solmi, M., Lingiardi, V. & Tanzilli, A. (2024)

**Harmonizing early intervention strategies: scoping review of clinical high risk for psychosis and borderline personality disorder.**

DOI: 10.3389/fpsyg.2024.1381864

## How much weight it bears: moderate

A pre-registered (OSF 8mz7a), PRISMA-ScR/JBI-compliant scoping review of three databases to August 2023, with independent dual title/abstract and full-text screening and third-judge adjudication, mapping 33 primary studies into four clinical-research paradigms. It can establish what the clinical high-risk literature actually measures and with which instruments (CAARMS, SIPS, SPI-A, ERIraos), that CHR-P is operationalised as a help-seeking, subthreshold, functionally impaired state rather than a diagnosis, and that transdiagnostic 'clinical staging' and 'pluripotency' models (CHARMS) are now competing with the psychosis-specific risk paradigm. It cannot establish prevalence, effect size or any causal claim: a scoping review by design does not appraise the quality of the studies it maps or pool their results, and the authors state as their first limitation that 'our study design did not allow for the development of clinical guidelines'. Treated as current rather than foundational: published June 2024, searched to August 2023.

*Graded on full-appraisal.*
