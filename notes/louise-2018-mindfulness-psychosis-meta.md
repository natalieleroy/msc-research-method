# Louise, S., Fitzpatrick, M., Strauss, C., Rossell, S. L. & Thomas, N. (2018)

**Mindfulness- and acceptance-based interventions for psychosis: our current understanding and a meta-analysis.**

DOI: 10.1016/j.schres.2017.05.023

## How much weight it bears: moderate

A meta-analysis of randomised controlled trials of third-wave (mindfulness, acceptance and compassion) interventions in psychosis, with psychotic symptoms as the pre-specified primary outcome and subgroup analysis separating group mindfulness-based work from individual ACT. It establishes that a usable randomised evidence base for mindfulness in psychosis EXISTS, and that its pooled effect on symptoms is small: g = 0.29 overall, with g = 0.46 for group mindfulness-based interventions and g = 0.08 for individual ACT-based ones. It cannot carry more than that: only 10 trials were pooled, there were no significant effects on positive symptoms, negative symptoms, hallucination distress or functioning, the authors state that further research is needed to determine the efficacy of specific treatment models, and the paper is closed access so the forest plots, risk-of-bias table and trial countries have NOT been read. Moderate rather than strong on the small number of trials, the null secondary outcomes and the abstract-only basis.

*Graded on design-only.*
