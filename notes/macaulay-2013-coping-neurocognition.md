# MacAulay, R. & Cohen, A. S. (2013)

**Affecting coping: does neurocognition predict approach and avoidant coping strategies within schizophrenia spectrum disorders?**

DOI: 10.1016/j.psychres.2013.04.004

## How much weight it bears: indicative

A single-site cross-sectional group comparison with a small clinical cell (27 patients) and a schizotypy group three times its size, read from the abstract only. It can establish an association between trait affect and self-reported coping style across schizophrenia-spectrum groups, and it reports that neurocognition did not predict coping style - a null result the authors did not expect. It cannot establish that coping ability causes or precedes symptom exacerbation, because nothing in it is longitudinal and symptom exacerbation is not an outcome it measures. Indicative because of the design, the cell sizes and abstract-only access.

*Graded on design-only.*
