# Marconi, A., Di Forti, M., Lewis, C. M., Murray, R. M. & Vassos, E. (2016)

**Meta-analysis of the Association Between the Level of Cannabis Use and Risk of Psychosis.**

DOI: 10.1093/schbul/sbw003

## How much weight it bears: strong

A systematic review of 571 references down to 18 studies, with 10 pooled by a random-effects method that simulates individual data points from the published summary data in order to fit a dose-response model, across 66,816 individuals. It can establish a graded association between level of cannabis use and psychosis risk, with an odds ratio of 3.90 (95% CI 2.84-5.34) for the heaviest users against non-users. It cannot establish causation and the authors say so explicitly, and the simulation step means the dose-response curve is reconstructed from summary data rather than observed, which is a real inferential cost.

*Graded on design-only.*
