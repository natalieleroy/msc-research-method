# Marriner, L., Larkin, M., Urquhart Law, G. & Kaur Bhogal, S. (2025)

**'I'm ruined': Young people's and their mothers' experiences of living with, and receiving a diagnosis of, borderline personality disorder: An interpretative phenomenological analysis**

DOI: 10.1111/papt.12549

## How much weight it bears: moderate

A sound primary qualitative study: IPA following the Smith, Flowers and Larkin six-step procedure, a genuinely novel multi-perspectival dyadic design, research-supervision triangulation of codes and themes, NHS research ethics approval, and participants offered sight of their transcripts to withdraw material - four did. It can establish what living with and being labelled with a borderline personality disorder diagnosis is like for a small number of English adolescents and their mothers. **It is not about psychosis**, and it cannot carry any claim about psychotic experience, onset or first episode. Moderate on its own terms; the reason it is not useful here is fit, not quality, which is why `serves` is empty.

*Graded on full-appraisal.*
