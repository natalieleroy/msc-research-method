# McCrone, P., Singh, S. P., Knapp, M., Smith, J., Clark, M., Shiers, D. & Tiffin, P. A. (2013)

**The economic impact of early intervention in psychosis services for children and adolescents.**

DOI: 10.1111/eip.12024

## How much weight it bears: moderate

A transparent decision model with a sensitivity analysis that can establish that early intervention in psychosis for under-18s plausibly produces short-term direct healthcare savings, driven by reduced in-patient length of stay, and that the base-case result was robust to varying each parameter by 50%. It cannot establish clinical effectiveness, because it measures cost and not outcome, and it is a model rather than a trial — the authors say so: 'like trials, decision models represent simplifications of reality'. Its cost figures are 2009-11 UK prices and are therefore fifteen years out of date as money, though the direction of the argument survives. Tier is moderate: sound method, stated limitations, single-region data, non-clinical outcome.
