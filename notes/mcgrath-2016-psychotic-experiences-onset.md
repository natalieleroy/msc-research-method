**Age of Onset and Lifetime Projected Risk of Psychotic Experiences: Cross-National Data From the World Mental Health Survey.**

DOI: 10.1093/schbul/sbw011

## How much weight it bears: moderate

A large cross-national probability-sample survey (n=31,261, 18 countries) using a two-part actuarial method to project lifetime risk, which can establish the shape of the age-of-onset distribution for psychotic experiences in the general population. It cannot speak to diagnosed psychotic disorder or to first-episode psychosis, and the onset ages are retrospective self-report, so recall error is built in. Moderate rather than strong because the design is cross-sectional with a modelled lifetime projection rather than prospective follow-up.

*Graded on design-only.*
