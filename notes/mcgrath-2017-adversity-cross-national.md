**The association between childhood adversities and subsequent first onset of psychotic experiences: a cross-national analysis of 23 998 respondents from 17 countries.**

DOI: 10.1017/S0033291716003263

## How much weight it bears: strong

Multi-stage clustered area probability samples in 17 countries with a weighted average response rate of 71.9%, analysed with discrete-time survival models that order adversity before the onset of psychotic experiences and adjust for prior-onset DSM-IV disorders. It can establish that the adversity-psychosis-experience association is not confined to Western samples and is not simply an artefact of comorbid disorder. It cannot establish causation or speak to diagnosed psychotic disorder, because respondents screening positive for possible psychotic disorder were deliberately excluded and the outcome is self-reported psychotic experiences.

*Graded on full-appraisal.*
