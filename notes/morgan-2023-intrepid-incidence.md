# Morgan, C., Cohen, A., Esponda, G. M., Roberts, T., John, S., Pow, J. L., Donald, C., Olley, B., Ayinde, O., Lam, J., Poornachandrika, P., Dazzan, P., Gaughran, F., Kannan, P. **Epidemiology of Untreated Psychoses in 3 Diverse Settings in the Global South: The International Research Program on Psychotic Disorders in Diverse Settings (INTREPID II).**

DOI: 10.1001/jamapsychiatry.2022.3781

## How much weight it bears: strong

A population-based incidence study over 3,790,697 person-years in three Global South catchment areas, using identical instruments (SCAN, PPHS) and case-finding across the professional, folk and popular sectors, with leakage studies and interrater reliability reported. It can establish that rates and the demographic and clinical shape of psychosis vary markedly by setting, and that the Global North sex-and-age pattern does not hold everywhere. It cannot establish incidence in the strict sense — the outcome is rate of *untreated* psychosis, a proxy — and the authors say rates where duration of untreated psychosis is long, as in Kancheepuram, are the least accurate proxies.

*Graded on full-appraisal.*
