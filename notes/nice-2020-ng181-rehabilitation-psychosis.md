# National Institute for Health and Care Excellence (2020)

**Rehabilitation for adults with complex psychosis. NICE guideline NG181.**

DOI: not reported

## How much weight it bears: strong

A national guideline developed through NICE's documented process, with named evidence reviews cited against each recommendation group (reviews A, C, E, F, G, I and P), covering a territory CG178 barely touches: rehabilitation pathways, supported accommodation, structured daily and group activity, supported employment, and team formulation. For the question 'what does rehabilitation actually consist of, and on whose authority', it is the UK authority and the only source in this corpus that addresses housing at all. It establishes the expected CONTENT of a rehabilitation and recovery plan - supported accommodation, daily living skills, structured group social and leisure activity, IPS-model supported employment, recovery colleges, and mindfulness among the additional psychological interventions - at a level a short plan can cite in one go. It establishes NOTHING about efficacy: it is a consensus synthesis, and it is scoped to COMPLEX psychosis, meaning treatment-resistant symptoms with impaired functioning, so it is the wrong citation for a first-episode or recent-onset case without saying so explicitly.
