# Panayi, P., Varese, F., Peters, E., Bentall, R., Hardy, A., Berry, K., Sellwood, W., Underwood, R., Steel, C., Mason, L. & Elliott, R. (2026)

**Investigating the neuroanatomical underpinnings of complex PTSD in people with psychosis.**

DOI: 10.1016/j.pscychresns.2026.112182

## How much weight it bears: moderate

A sound primary neuroimaging study that can establish that, within a psychosis population, those additionally meeting ICD-11 cPTSD criteria differ structurally in limbic and prefrontal regions — larger bilateral anterior insula and left parahippocampus, smaller left medial orbitofrontal cortex — with all differences bar hippocampal volume surviving whole-brain correction. It cannot establish direction or cause: it is cross-sectional, and the authors state they cannot tell whether differences reflect cPTSD itself or complex trauma exposure. Antipsychotic and other psychotropic exposure is a stated confounder and differed between groups. Tier is moderate, not strong: n=108 across two separately acquired samples on three different scanners, with correlational post-hoc analyses described by the authors only as trends.
