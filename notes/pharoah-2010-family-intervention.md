# Pharoah, F., Mari, J., Rathbone, J. & Wong, W. (2010)

**Family intervention for schizophrenia.**

DOI: 10.1002/14651858.CD000088.pub2

## How much weight it bears: moderate

A Cochrane review of 53 RCTs with a very large pooled sample, giving relative risks with 95% confidence intervals and numbers needed to treat, and reporting a substantial reduction in relapse (RR 0.55, 95% CI 0.5 to 0.6, NNT 7) alongside improved medication adherence and reduced expressed emotion. It can establish that family intervention is an evidence-based component of a psychosis treatment plan, and it supplies the NNT a plan can be justified with. It CANNOT be read at face value: the authors state the treatment effects 'may be overestimated due to the poor methodological quality' of the trials, the hospital-admission result crosses 1 (RR 0.78, 95% CI 0.6 to 1.0), and they note small negative studies may have been missed by the search. It is also NOT first-episode-specific — the population is schizophrenia and schizophrenia-like conditions broadly — and it is a 2010 review, so fifteen years out of date. Moderate rather than strong on the authors' own quality caveat and on fit.

*Graded on design-only.*
