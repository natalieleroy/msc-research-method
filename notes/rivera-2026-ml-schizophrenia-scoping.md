# Rivera, L. A., Costa-Dookhan, K. A., Raj, V., Dissanayake, J., Siddiqui, I., Hahn, M., Rotenberg, M., Maslej, M. & Foussias, G. (2026)

**Machine learning methods and schizophrenia spectrum disorders: a scoping review.**

DOI: 10.1136/bmjopen-2025-112849

## How much weight it bears: moderate

A PRISMA-ScR scoping review with a librarian-designed search, dual independent screening and extraction with third-reviewer adjudication and 5% calibration, mapping 88 machine-learning studies in schizophrenia spectrum disorders across five outcome domains, and appraising every one of them with APPRAISE-AI, an instrument built for AI studies supporting clinical decisions. Its most useful finding is about the literature rather than from it: a mean quality score of 47.4 out of 100, with the two weakest domains being robustness of results (mean 9.1/20) and methodological conduct (mean 6.2/20), and most studies either not addressing generalisability at all or naming it only as a limitation. It cannot establish how well any of these models perform: a scoping review by design does not pool, the authors say performance was reported too heterogeneously to synthesise, and the figures they quote are illustrative single studies. Rated moderate rather than strong: the design precludes effect estimates, inclusion was English-language and peer-reviewed only with no grey literature, 70.5% of included studies were cross-sectional, and the paper contains two internal inconsistencies recorded in the reader's note below.
