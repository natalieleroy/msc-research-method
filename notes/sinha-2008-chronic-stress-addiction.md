# Sinha, R. (2008)

**Chronic stress, drug use, and vulnerability to addiction.**

DOI: 10.1196/annals.1441.030

## How much weight it bears: indicative

A single-author narrative review that can establish the shape of the stress-addiction vulnerability account — that chronic stress, child maltreatment and accumulated adversity alter CRF/HPA, autonomic and central noradrenergic systems, and that these alterations act on corticostriatal-limbic motivational and learning systems — and can identify where the primary evidence sits. It cannot establish any specific causal pathway, has no search strategy, and is eighteen years old. Used as FOUNDATIONAL framing for a stress-vulnerability model, not as a current statement of the addiction neuroscience literature. Tier is indicative: authoritative review, no primary data, substantially dated.
