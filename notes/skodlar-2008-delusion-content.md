# Škodlar, B., Dernovšek, M. Z. & Kocmur, M. (2008)

**Psychopathology of schizophrenia in Ljubljana (Slovenia) from 1881 to 2000: changes in the content of delusions in schizophrenia patients related to various sociopolitical, technical and scientific changes**

DOI: 10.1177/0020764007083875

## How much weight it bears: indicative

A retrospective case-note study of 120 randomly selected first-admission records spanning 120 years, which can show that the thematic content of delusions shifted alongside the arrival of radio and television and alongside political change in Slovenia. It cannot establish that the technology caused the shift, nor that the comparison is like for like, because diagnostic practice, recording conventions and the hospital's catchment all changed across the same period and roughly 20 records represent each 20-year span. Indicative rather than moderate because of the small per-period cell sizes and because the appraisal rests on the abstract alone.

*Graded on design-only.*
