# Song, J., Pasman, J. A., Johansson, V., Kuja-Halkola, R., Harder, A., Karlsson, R., Lu, Y., Kowalec, K., Pedersen, N. L., Cannon, T. D., Hultman, C. M. & Sullivan, P. F. (2024)

**Polygenic Risk Scores and Twin Concordance for Schizophrenia and Bipolar Disorder.**

DOI: 10.1001/jamapsychiatry.2024.2406

## How much weight it bears: moderate

A twin study that brings two independent methods to bear on the same question — classical twin modelling and molecular polygenic risk scores — in a national twin registry, which is why it can speak to heritability and to whether the molecular and twin estimates agree. It can establish a twin heritability estimate for psychosis of 0.73 and show that polygenic risk predicted co-twin concordance. It cannot support a precise heritability figure: the genotyped sample is small at 292 twins and the 95% confidence interval on the headline estimate runs from 0.30 to 1.00, which is almost the whole admissible range.

*Graded on design-only.*
