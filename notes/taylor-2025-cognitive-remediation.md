DOI: 10.1177/01454455251343303

## How much weight it bears: indicative

An advocacy-framed expert overview, current at 2025, that can establish that cognitive remediation is treated in the field as an evidence-based intervention, that it uses therapeutic formulation in the way other psychological therapies do, and that it is nonetheless inconsistently included in clinical guidelines. It cannot carry an effect size or a recovery claim of its own, because the authors state plainly that they 'have not systematically reviewed the literature but have used expert knowledge and reviews by others'. The authors are among the field's principal proponents, which is relevant to how its conclusions are read. Tier is indicative: a non-systematic review with a stated position.
