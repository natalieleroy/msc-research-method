# Torres-Rodríguez, A., Griffiths, M. D. & Carbonell, X. (2018)

**The treatment of Internet Gaming Disorder: a brief overview of the PIPATIC program.**

DOI: 10.1007/s11469-017-9825-0

## How much weight it bears: indicative

A programme-description paper that can establish what a manualised multi-module adolescent intervention looks like in practice — six modules over six months and twenty-two 45-minute weekly sessions for both individual and family work, designed against CONSORT reporting standards — and is therefore usable as a TEMPLATE for the architecture of a treatment plan. It cannot establish that the programme works: the authors' own word for their outcome data is 'preliminary', and no controlled comparison is reported in this paper. Its population is adolescents with Internet Gaming Disorder, not psychosis, so the FIT is structural rather than evidential. Tier is indicative: a design paper with preliminary, uncontrolled outcome data.

*Graded on full-appraisal.*
