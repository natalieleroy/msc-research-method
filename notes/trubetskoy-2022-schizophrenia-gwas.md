# Trubetskoy, V., Pardiñas, A. F., Qi, T. and 300 further authors, Schizophrenia Working Group of the Psychiatric Genomics Consortium (2022)

**Mapping genomic loci implicates genes and synaptic biology in schizophrenia.**

DOI: 10.1038/s41586-022-04434-5

## How much weight it bears: strong

A two-stage genome-wide association study of up to 76,755 cases and 243,649 controls reporting 287 distinct genome-wide significant loci, with fine-mapping and functional genomic follow-up prioritising 120 genes, published in Nature. It can establish that schizophrenia liability is highly polygenic, that the associated variation is concentrated in genes expressed in central-nervous-system neurons rather than other tissues, and that common and rare variant signals converge. It cannot establish individual-level risk prediction, cannot identify a cause in any one person, and because the heritability figure of 60-80% that opens the paper is cited from prior twin work rather than estimated here, that figure is a secondary citation if used.

*Graded on design-only.*
