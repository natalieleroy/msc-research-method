# Varese, F., Smeets, F., Drukker, M., Lieverse, R., Lataster, T., Viechtbauer, W., Read, J., van Os, J. & Bentall, R. P. (2012)

**Childhood adversities increase the risk of psychosis: a meta-analysis of patient-control, prospective- and cross-sectional cohort studies.**

DOI: 10.1093/schbul/sbs050

## How much weight it bears: strong

A meta-analysis pooling three different observational designs, which can establish that the adversity-psychosis association is large, consistent and not an artefact of any single design: the overall OR was 2.78 (95% CI 2.34-3.31) and it held separately in prospective studies (OR 2.75, 95% CI 2.17-3.47). It cannot establish causation, cannot separate the timing or dose of adversity, and the authors state that heterogeneity was substantial for every outcome. The tier is strong because it is a design-triangulated meta-analysis with sensitivity analyses restricted to adjusted estimates, not because of its size alone.

*Graded on full-appraisal.*
