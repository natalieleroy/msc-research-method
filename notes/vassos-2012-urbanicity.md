# Vassos, E., Pedersen, C. B., Murray, R. M., Collier, D. A. & Lewis, C. M. (2012)

**Meta-analysis of the association of urbanicity with schizophrenia.**

DOI: 10.1093/schbul/sbs096

## How much weight it bears: moderate

A meta-analysis that reframes the urbanicity-schizophrenia association as a linear dose-response on the log odds rather than a most-urban-versus-most-rural comparison, estimating risk in the most urban environment as 2.37 times that in the most rural. It can establish that the association is graded by degree of urbanicity rather than being a city-versus-country dichotomy, which is what makes it useful for a predisposing-factor argument. It cannot establish causation or distinguish social causation from social drift, and the abstract does not state how many studies were included or give a confidence interval for the headline 2.37 figure, so the precision of that estimate cannot be judged from this record.

*Graded on design-only.*
