# Vinogradov, S., Chafee, M. V., Lee, E. & Morishita, H. (2023)

**Psychosis spectrum illnesses as disorders of prefrontal critical period plasticity.**

DOI: 10.1038/s41386-022-01451-w

## How much weight it bears: indicative

A selective narrative review that can establish the existence and shape of the adolescent-prefrontal-critical-period account of psychosis, and is the most current statement of it in this corpus, but cannot evidence any specific causal claim: the authors themselves write that 'the evidence supporting each of these themes is indirect'. It is useful precisely where it is honest — it states that microcircuit function cannot be assessed directly in humans and that macrocircuit plasticity can only be inferred. The tier is indicative because it is a theory synthesis with no primary data, selective rather than systematic in its search.

*Graded on full-appraisal.*
