# Weyandt, L. L., Clarkin, C. M., Holding, E. Z., May, S. E., Marraccini, M. E., Gudmundsdottir, B. G., Shepard, E. & Thompson, L. (2020)

**Neuroplasticity in children and adolescents in response to treatment intervention: A systematic review of the literature.**

DOI: 10.1177/2514183X20974231

## How much weight it bears: moderate

A PRISMA-guided systematic review of 28 neuroimaging studies that can establish that experience-dependent interventions are associated with measurable neuroplastic change in children and adolescents across both neurotypical and clinical populations, and that the construct of neuroplasticity is operationalised inconsistently across that literature. It cannot establish that any particular intervention works or that neuroplastic change corresponds to clinical improvement: the authors state it 'remains unclear' whether the molecular changes map onto cognition and behaviour, and that methodological limitations constrain clinical utility. None of the 28 studies is on psychosis as far as the review's own description goes, so the fit here is to the critical-period argument rather than to any psychosis claim. Tier is moderate: systematic method, substantial heterogeneity, stated limits.
