# World Health Organization (2019)

**International classification of diseases for mortality and morbidity statistics. 11th revision (ICD-11)**

DOI: not reported

## How much weight it bears: strong

An international consensus classification adopted by the 72nd World Health Assembly in 2019 and in effect since 1 January 2022: for the question 'whose definition is this and what does it say', a standard is the primary source and nothing outranks it. It establishes diagnostic nomenclature and criteria, and it is the right citation for a definition, for the boundary of a diagnostic category, and for the statement that something is or is not a recognised diagnostic entity - which Essay 2 needs. It establishes nothing empirical: a classification carries no prevalence, no effect and no mechanism, and the consensus process behind it is not an evidence synthesis. Strong for definitional use only; the tier would be meaningless applied to any other use.
