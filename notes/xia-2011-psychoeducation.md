# Xia, J., Merinder, L. B. & Belgamwar, M. R. (2011)

**Psychoeducation for schizophrenia.**

DOI: 10.1002/14651858.CD002831.pub2

## How much weight it bears: moderate

A Cochrane review of 44 RCTs and 5142 participants reporting that psychoeducation reduces non-compliance (RR 0.52, 95% CI 0.40 to 0.67, NNT 11), relapse (RR 0.70, 95% CI 0.61 to 0.81, NNT 9) and readmission (RR 0.71, 95% CI 0.56 to 0.89, NNT 5), with better social and global functioning and higher service satisfaction. It can establish that patient education is a treatment component with trial evidence behind it rather than a courtesy, which is what `e2-plan` needs. It CANNOT be taken at the size reported: the authors state plainly that 'the true size of effect is likely to be less than demonstrated in this review', risk of bias was rated moderate, the trials are mostly inpatient and median duration is only about 12 weeks, and the register search ends in February 2010. Moderate on the authors' own effect-size caveat and the inpatient setting, which is a poor match for a college student in the community.

*Graded on design-only.*
