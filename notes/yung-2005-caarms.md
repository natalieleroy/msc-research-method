# Yung, A. R., Yuen, H. P., McGorry, P. D., Phillips, L. J., Kelly, D., Dell'Olio, M., Francey, S. M., Cosgrave, E. M., Killackey, E., Stanford, C., Godfrey, K. & Buckby, J. (2005)

**Mapping the onset of psychosis: the Comprehensive Assessment of At-Risk Mental States.**

DOI: 10.1080/j.1440-1614.2005.01714.x

## How much weight it bears: indicative

The paper that introduced the CAARMS, reporting what its own authors twice call a pilot and preliminary evaluation: concurrent, discriminant and predictive validity plus inter-rater reliability, tested in an ultra high risk group, a control group and 150 non-psychotic help-seekers followed for six months. It can establish that the instrument exists, what it is for (assessing psychopathology indicating imminent first-episode psychosis, and determining whether a person meets ultra high risk criteria), and that CAARMS-positive help-seekers transitioned at a higher rate than CAARMS-negative ones. It cannot establish how much higher with any useful precision: the relative risk of 12.44 carries a 95% confidence interval of 1.5 to 103.41, a range spanning nearly two orders of magnitude, and the follow-up was six months. Rated indicative rather than moderate on the authors' own description of it as a pilot, on that interval, and because the full text could not be reached, so the UHR and control group sizes, the transition counts and the reliability coefficients are all unverified.

*Graded on design-only.*
